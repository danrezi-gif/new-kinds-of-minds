Our members

Autism-Europe is the only European organisation gathering almost 90 national and regional associations advocating for the rights of autistic people from 40 European countries, including 26 from the European Union.

Autism-Europe operates as an umbrella organisation, representing an estimated 5 million people throughout Europe. It brings together almost 90 member associations, individual members and/or self-advocates advocating for the rights of autistic people from 40 European countries and beyond.
