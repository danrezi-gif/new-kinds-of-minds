Eighty-eight percent of the budget pays people, participation, artistic production, accessibility, and translation. Infrastructure is small because it already exists: the site is a static export, the dataset is a public file, and the submissions service runs on a shared twelve-dollar server.

Nothing is funded retroactively. The platform and its first 102 entries were built before this application; the grant buys the collective year of work that a prototype cannot do alone.

All payments to collaborators are fees for work performed during the project period, paid from the award on delivery. No personal expenses, debts, legal costs, or fundraising events are included. Exchange-rate variation is absorbed by the contingency line and, if needed, by the documentation line.
