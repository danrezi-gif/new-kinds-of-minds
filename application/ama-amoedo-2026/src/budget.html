Forty-five percent of the budget goes directly to Latin American artists and initiatives as fees for contributions and conversations; a further quarter pays for the sonic and visual environment and the interface that hold them. The platform and its first 471 entries exist already and are not funded retroactively.

All payments to contributors are fees for work created or licensed during the project period. No personal expenses, debts, legal costs, or fundraising events are included. Exchange-rate variation is absorbed by the contingency line.
