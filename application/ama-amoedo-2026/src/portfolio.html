Maps, instruments, and rooms for other kinds of minds

Fourteen images from six works, 2018 to 2026: a participatory atlas of neurodiversity, immersive environments for contemplative states, two instruments driven by quantum randomness, a ceremony of encounter, and a community for neurodivergent creators.

All works: concept, design, and development by Daniel Rezinovsky unless stated. Live links are given on each page.

New Kinds of Minds: A Global Atlas of Neurodiversity

2026, ongoing · participatory digital cartography, open dataset · new-kinds-of-minds-hub.vercel.app

A living map of the organizations, collectives, artistic practices, research groups, and peer communities through which societies are learning to recognize different kinds of minds. Each entry is verified against its source and classified by kind, focus, and leadership; each kind of initiative is drawn with its own mark, so a region reads as a pattern of forms. First public release: 445 initiatives in 87 countries, an index, permanent entry pages, a public contribution form, and the dataset under an open license.

The work proposed to the Foundation is Phase I of this atlas: a year of mapping Latin America in Portuguese and Spanish with neurodivergent collaborators.

Role: concept, research method, taxonomy, visual system, development, editorial verification of the first 445 entries.

New Kinds of Minds, continued

About, index, and data pages

The map is one view. Everything on it is also a sortable table and a permanent page, because a neurodiversity project cannot make the map the only way in. The About page is the manifesto: why map this movement, why geography matters, how records are made, and where the categories fail. The Data page publishes the schema, the taxonomy with its known problems, and the whole dataset for download.

Role: writing, information architecture, accessibility design.

Ontik

2025 to 2026 · WebXR environments, Three.js and GLSL · ontik.app

Immersive environments for contemplative and non-ordinary states, playable in a VR headset or a browser: a nine-minute experience and five shorter portals, each a shader-built world with spatial sound. Ontik grew out of clinical work with people who had lived through spontaneous altered states and had no place to put them; the environments are attempts to build such places. The project's research side invites feedback from people with direct experience of mystical, psychedelic, or anomalous states.

Role: concept, shader and environment design, sound design, development. Sole author.

Quantum Sphere

2026 · real-time generative work driven by hardware quantum randomness · mindlamp.monkadelic.me

A raymarched sphere that breathes at rest and changes size and color with a live stream of quantum random numbers from a photon-detection device in a German laboratory. The piece computes a running statistic against the protocol of the Princeton Engineering Anomalies Research lab and visibly shifts state when the stream departs from chance, so that a viewer can sit with the oldest question in parapsychology, whether attention touches matter, as an image rather than a table. Each session is logged.

Role: concept, shader adaptation, statistics, development.

Quantum I Ching

2026 · divination instrument, English and Portuguese · iching.monkadelic.me

An I Ching that casts each line from three bits of hardware quantum randomness and reads the resulting hexagram through Richard Wilhelm's translation with a language model as reader. It is a small work about an old question, whether a random process can carry meaning, built so that the randomness is at least genuinely physical. The Portuguese edition brought the sixty-four hexagrams into Brazilian Portuguese.

Role: concept, interface, development, Portuguese edition.

Encontro

2026, in development · WebXR ceremony of encounter · danrezi-gif.github.io/encontro

A place to meet without a body. The visitor becomes a field of iridescent light that ripples with hand movement, contracts in stillness, and scatters when moved too fast. There are no avatars, names, or profiles. The first phase, presence, is public; the next phases, other fields and networked encounter between several people, are the reason the work exists and are being built. Encontro is the closest ancestor of the atlas: both are about what happens when minds that are usually alone find each other.

Role: concept, shader design, development.

Entrementes

2026, forming · bilingual project and community for neurodivergent people, artists, and therapists in Brazil · danrezi-gif.github.io/Entrementes

Entrementes is the community from which the atlas grew: a Portuguese and English project bringing together neurodivergent people, creative practitioners, and therapists around a view of neurodivergence as cognitive variation rather than deficit. Its first public forms are a media channel, a reading program, a workshop series on AI as a cognitive tool for neurodivergent creators, and the "Novas Mentes" program, which draws on my 2022 thesis on spiritual awakening. New Kinds of Minds is its first public instrument.

Role: founder, writer, developer.

Writing and research

2017 to 2023 · book, chapter, articles, thesis

Encontro com o Absoluto: uma jornada além das fronteiras da consciência (Coffeers, 2018) is a philosophical and contemplative account of the encounter between human consciousness and the ground of being, written from a spontaneous awakening I had at twenty-six. The MA thesis Transições Ontológicas (PUC-SP, 2022) examined spiritual awakening through phenomenology, philosophy of mind, and contemplative traditions, and was supported by a 2020 John Templeton Foundation grant in the Cognitive Psychology of Religion in Brazil program.

As a co-author I worked on the Brazilian Portuguese validation of the Mystical Experience Questionnaire (MEQ30) and the States of Consciousness Questionnaire (Archives of Clinical Psychiatry, 2017), and on a study of social support among people reporting mediumship experiences (International Journal of Latin American Religions, 2023). A book chapter on the philosophical dimensions of spiritual awakening for health professionals appeared in 2021.

Relevance: the atlas's verification discipline and its treatment of categories as historically contingent come from this research background, as does the habit of naming sources.

Where these works meet

Every piece here is a room or an instrument for a kind of mind that usually has nowhere to go: a contemplative state, an anomalous experience, a question put to randomness, a body that wants to meet without a body, a neurodivergent adult looking for others. The atlas is the same gesture at the scale of a movement. It draws the rooms that already exist, all over the world, and lets the people in them see each other.

Monkadelic (monkadelic.me) is the umbrella under which the immersive and generative works are published; danielrezinovsky.com holds the research and clinical side.
