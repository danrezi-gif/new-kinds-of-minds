Daniel Rezinovsky

Clinical psychologist, researcher of consciousness and unusual minds, maker of interactive and immersive works

Born Buenos Aires, 1984. Argentine and Brazilian. Lives in Brazil.
dr73@rice.edu · danielrezinovsky.com · monkadelic.me · github.com/danrezi-gif

Profile

I trained in neuroscience and then in clinical psychology, practised for twelve years in Curitiba with adults whose minds did not fit the categories I had been taught, and moved into the academic study of mystical and anomalous experience. Since 2019 the name New Kinds of Minds has covered my work on unusual minds: a video series on the psychology of artists, a book in progress, and now a participatory global atlas of the neurodiversity movement. I build interactive, immersive, and generative works about consciousness (Ontik, Quantum Sphere, Quantum I Ching, Encontro), founded Entrementes, a bilingual project for neurodivergent people, artists, and therapists in Brazil, and am completing a PhD in Religious Studies at Rice University on the phenomenology of mystical deification. Native Portuguese and Spanish, fluent English and French.

Interactive, immersive, and artistic works

2025 to 2026Ontik. WebXR contemplative environments built with Three.js and custom GLSL shaders: a nine-minute experience and shorter pieces, for Quest and desktop. Sole author. ontik.app
2026Quantum Sphere. Real-time raymarched sphere driven by a live hardware quantum random number stream, in the tradition of the PEAR Lab mind-matter experiments; logs each session for analysis. mindlamp.monkadelic.me

2026Encontro. WebXR ceremony of encounter in which the visitor becomes a field of light responding to hand movement; multi-user phase in development. danrezi-gif.github.io/encontro
2026Mu. A minimal voice-enabled AI conversation with a Zen aesthetic.
