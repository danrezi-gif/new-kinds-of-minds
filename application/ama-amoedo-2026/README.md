## Facts verified from the official rules

- Applicants: individuals, collectives, foundations, nonprofits, 18+, with a meaningful connection to Latin America by nationality, heritage, project site, or collaboration. Daniel qualifies by nationality (Argentine, Brazilian), residence (Brazil), and project site.
- Two grants of US$10,000 in this category; one year from the award to complete the project; projects start January 2027; single bank transfer; reports on request for twelve months.
- Application in Spanish, Portuguese, or English.
- Attachments: CV (no limit), previous-work PDF (max 20 captioned images), budget (Excel or PDF, by category), ID copy, optional annex, optional proof of legal registration (not required).
- Ineligible: personal expenses, debts, legal costs, fundraising events; commercial or state applicants.
- Jury 2026: Igor Simões, Jovanna Venegas, María Wills Londoño, Laura Hakel. Criteria: fit with the category, contribution to artistic development, feasibility, realistic budget; they favor proposals that "present challenges, invite reflection and experimentation, and demonstrate artistic rigor."
- 2025 grantees in this category: Alianza Wichí (Argentina), Nube Lab (Chile).
