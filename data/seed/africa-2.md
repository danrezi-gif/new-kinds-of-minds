# Africa depth, second pass

22 entries across 10 countries: Nigeria (6), Kenya (3), Ghana (1), South Africa (5), Uganda (2), Egypt (1), Namibia (1), Mauritius (1), Zimbabwe (1, PANPPD), Somalia (1, bonus — not in the original brief but a verified live centre worth the extra country).

This fell well short of the 35-45 target and the 18-country goal. The cause was tooling, not lack of leads: WebSearch hit its session-wide quota almost immediately (shared across concurrently running agents), and WebFetch against Google/Bing/DuckDuckGo returned CAPTCHA pages, cached unrelated results, or geolocation-mismatched pages rather than real search results. Facebook pages returned no content via WebFetch. That left direct domain-guessing plus curl verification, and a couple of directory pages that did work (autismaroundtheglobe.org's resource directory, Wikipedia's disability-by-country categories, panppd.org). Domain-guessing has a very low hit rate outside a handful of English-language markets with decent web presence (Nigeria, Kenya, South Africa), and essentially failed for every Francophone (Senegal, Cameroon, Côte d'Ivoire, DR Congo, Algeria, Tunisia, Morocco) and Lusophone (Angola, Mozambique) target, and for Ethiopia, Tanzania, Rwanda, Zambia, Malawi, Botswana and Sudan.

Notable finds: PANPPD (Pan African Network for Persons with Psychosocial Disabilities), an explicitly nd-led continental network active in 20+ countries — its own member roster wasn't published on the pages I could reach, so it stands in for the "PANPPD members" brief item rather than fulfilling it fully. BasicNeeds/BNBR turned out to be a live cross-border network (confirmed working sites in both Kenya and Ghana; Uganda and Tanzania country sites did not resolve).

Sites confirmed dead/hijacked and dropped: Nigeria's APAI (apai.org.ng, DNS failure) and the alternate Nigerian Autistic Society domain (.com); Kenya's USP Kenya (uspkenya.org now redirects to an unrelated commercial site — likely an expired, squatted domain); Ghana's MindFreedom Ghana (mindfreedomghana.org redirects to a spam/survey site); South Africa's ADHASA (adhasa.co.za now serves generic "learning theory" content, not the ADHD association) and Dyslexia SA (dyslexia.org.za is now a domains.co.za parking page); Egypt's ESA (esaegypt.com parked) and Al Nour Center (empty JS shell, likely an unfinished AI-website-builder site). Nigerian Autistic Society itself is kept in the file with a note, since its history and centres are corroborated by a third-party directory even though its own domain no longer resolves.

Unverified leads I could not chase down in time: Autism Society of Kenya and Autism Foundation of Kenya (real per multiple directories, no working domain found); Kaizora Foundation Kenya (site resolves but has almost no content — couldn't confirm its focus area); Ghana's Autism Ambassadors Ghana and Special Mothers Project (Facebook-only presence, unreadable via available tools); any Ethiopian, Tanzanian, Rwandan or Zambian autism/neurodiversity organisation (several names surfaced in a directory with phone numbers only, no verifiable web presence); Morocco's Collectif Autisme Maroc and Tunisia's Association Tunisienne d'Aide aux Autistes (names plausible from general knowledge, no reachable site found under several domain guesses).

Entries I was least sure about: She Writes Woman and MANI (Nigeria) — both marked "mixed" leadership rather than "nd-led" since founders have discussed personal lived experience but the organisations don't explicitly self-describe as survivor-led; PANPPD's Zimbabwe location, inferred only from a +263 contact number, not stated on the site.
