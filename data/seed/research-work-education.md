# research-work-education.json — report

45 entries across 18 countries: Argentina, Australia, Belgium, Brazil, Canada, Finland, France, Germany, Ireland, Italy, Japan, Mexico, Norway, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, United Kingdom, United States. By type: research 13, education 9, work 23.

WebSearch was unavailable for almost the entire session (shared session budget was already spent by sibling agents), so verification relied on curl (with a full browser user agent), Wikipedia page fetches, and direct navigation of known institutional/company sites — as the second-pass brief anticipated. Several guessed domains turned out to be GoDaddy parking pages disguised as real sites (a JS redirect to `/lander` with `ap:"parking"` in the payload) rather than actual organisations — this caught out `actionforautism.org`, `untapped.group`, and `neurodiversityinstitute.org`, all dropped. Roim Rachok (Israel), Rodin Remediation Academy, Fundación Kimen (Chile, resolved but served an empty body), CRAE at UCL (every guessed URL path 404'd), and a Ghent University disability-studies page for Geert Van Hove could not be pinned to a live, verifiable URL and were dropped rather than guessed.

The auticon network supplied ten of the work entries (Germany, UK, US, France, Italy, Switzerland, Australia, Canada, Norway, Finland) since the brief explicitly asked for country-by-country coverage and each national path (auticon.com/de, /uk, etc.) verified independently with its own 200 response. Passwerk (Belgium) has rebranded to TAKTO — confirmed by the redirect and by "Passwerk" and "autisme" still appearing in the new site's copy. Unicus Norway has been absorbed into auticon (unicus.no now redirects to auticon.com/no); only Unicus Sweden remains independent.

Six entries were dropped mid-build after `node scripts/build-dataset.mjs` flagged duplicate URLs against sibling files written concurrently: `instituto-rodrigo-mendes` (also in brazil-2), `fundacja-synapsis` (also in europe-autism-federations), `auticon-us` and `extraordinary-ventures` (also in north-america-2), and `neurodiversity-hub`/`kaien` (also in asia-pacific-2). Five replacement entries (TEACCH, Autism Science Foundation, Autisme Centraal, Gautena, National Autism Center) were added afterward to bring the count back to 45; the final build ran clean with no errors.

Leads found but not verified in time: Autism&Uni (EU project, likely archived, no live successor site found), Frankfurt-area autism research group, a CONICET-specific (rather than PANAACEA-network) autism lab in Argentina, a UNAM-affiliated autism research lab in Mexico, and Autistic Doctors International's precise founding date/location (attributed to Dr Mary Doherty, an Irish autistic anaesthetist, based on prior knowledge rather than a page citation — flagged for a moderator to double-check).

Leadership fields were kept conservative: most employment social enterprises are marked "ally-led" (auticon, Aspiritech, Rising Tide, Extraordinary Ventures, Kaien) based on known founder biographies rather than an explicit "led by" site statement, and several research centres are "unknown" where no governance claim was found. Only Ultranauts, Autistic Doctors International, and the Tojisha-Kenkyu Network carry "nd-led" on the strength of explicit self-description or defining methodology.
