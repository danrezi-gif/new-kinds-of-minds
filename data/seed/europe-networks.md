# Europe networks — harvest report

37 entries across 3 umbrella-federation member lists (EUCAP, ADHD Europe, European Dyslexia Association) plus five directly-searched national organisations, spanning 19 countries/territories: Czechia, Spain, Portugal, Italy, Denmark, United Kingdom (England + Scotland), Germany, Belgium, Estonia, Sweden, Finland, Slovakia, Romania, Netherlands, Poland, Ireland, Jersey, Åland Islands, Austria, France.

**EUCAP (18 entries).** Pulled the full and associate member tables from eucap.eu/members/. All full members and most associate members explicitly self-describe as run by or for autistic people ("autisternes egen forening", "all full members are autistic", "von Autist*innen für Autist*innen") and are marked `nd-led` on that basis. Three — MTÜ Autistid (Estonia), Suomen Autismikirjon Yhdistys (Finland), A Centrum (Slovakia) — are marked `unknown` because their own pages, unlike the others, never state a leadership composition, even though EUCAP membership implies it. Two redirects were followed to their live domains: eany.ee → autistid.ee, arghscotland.co.uk → arghscotland.org.

**ADHD Europe (5 entries).** The members page only rendered its "2025 newest" table (ADHD Jersey, Akrasia/Czechia, Fundacja ADHD/Poland, NPF Åland, Scottish ADHD Coalition); the linked full historical member directory did not return list content via fetch. All five are `mixed` or `unknown` — none claims to be strictly ADHD-adult-led.

**European Dyslexia Association (10 entries).** Selected one member per country for geographic spread from EDA's country-by-country member table (Austria, Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, UK, Ireland, Italy, Poland, Sweden). None of these state a leadership composition beyond boards/chairs, so all are `unknown` except FFDys (France), which explicitly says it mobilises "parents and professionals."

**Additional targeted searches (4 entries).** AsIAm (Ireland, nd-led, ≥50% autistic board policy), Scottish Autism (ally-led anchor, founded 1968 by parents), Autismus-Kultur (Germany, nd-led perspective site), Fundacja Autism Team (Poland, mixed). Could not verify a separate Portuguese "Autistas Adultos" org (EUCAP's actual Portuguese full member is Associação Portuguesa Voz do Autista, used instead); could not verify a Welsh organisation still active (Autism Cymru deregistered 2019, National Autistic Society Cymru is a branch of the UK-wide NAS rather than an independent entity, so Wales was left out this round); did not find an independent Berlin-based German research co-op site for the Autismus-Forschungs-Kooperation mentioned in secondary sources.

**Uncertain/flagged in `notes`:** Autisme- og Aspergerforeningen (Denmark) returns a non-standard HTTP 454 to curl from this network — verified live via fetch instead. Autismus-Kultur and Adventor both 403 bare curl requests (bot-blocking) but are reachable with a browser header and were read via fetch. Autminds' founding year (2015) is inferred from its own "11 years" claim ahead of an announced final October-2026 congress.

**Leads not verified in time:** ADHD Georgia and Slovenia's Mavrični Bojevniki (both Facebook-only, no independent site); the Autism Research Cooperation (AFK) Berlin research group; Wales-specific autistic-led organisations; a wider pull of ADHD Europe's full historical member directory beyond the 2025 cohort.
