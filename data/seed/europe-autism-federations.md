# Europe autism federations — harvest report

**30 entries, 26 countries:** Poland (3), Czechia, Slovakia, Hungary (2), Romania, Bulgaria, Croatia, Serbia, Slovenia, Greece, Cyprus, Malta, Lithuania, Latvia, Estonia, Finland (2), Sweden, Iceland, Luxembourg, Switzerland, Ukraine, Moldova, Albania, Turkey, Austria, Andorra.

**Method:** Autism-Europe's members page (`/who-we-are/our-members/`) renders its directory as an interactive map with no static HTML content, so individual `/blog/member/<slug>/` pages were also dead ends (WordPress custom post type not exposed via REST, content loaded by JS). The page does link a downloadable PDF, "Members-associations-of-Autism-Europe_Aug2026.pdf", listing every full, affiliated and associated member with postal address and website — this became the primary directory. Each listed site was then verified live with curl (full browser UA) and, where reachable, read with WebFetch for founding year, city and leadership self-description. WebSearch was unavailable for this session (budget exhausted), so Austria (not an Autism-Europe member at all) was found by educated domain guessing (`autistenhilfe.at`) verified independently, and Georgia, Armenia, North Macedonia, Bosnia and Montenegro could not be filled — no AE listing exists for them and no search tool was available to find independent national societies, so they were dropped rather than guessed.

**Leadership:** true to the brief's expectation, nearly every entry here is ally-led (parent-founded) or unknown; none is nd-led. Most describe themselves as founded by "a group of parents" — Croatia (1979), Latvia (2006), Moldova (2008) — or simply don't state governance on their sites (roughly half marked "unknown" rather than guessed).

**Entries I was unsure about:**
- **Autism Today Association (Bulgaria)** — its core activity is ABA therapy delivery, which sits close to the brief's "avoid ABA providers" guidance. Kept because it is Bulgaria's only AE-affiliated member found and does public advocacy too, but flagged for review.
- **Albanian Children Foundation** — a broad child-health charity (also covers thalassemia) rather than an autism-only body; its National Center for Autism program is real but not its whole remit. Kept for Albania's country coverage.
- **AOSZ (Hungary)** and **Zveza NVO za avtizem Slovenije (Slovenia)** — both confirmed live (200 responses) but their own sites gave no readable content (a bare landing page and a bot-verification wall respectively), so facts rely on the Autism-Europe PDF rather than the org's own words.
- **AUTEA (Andorra)** — site returned HTTP 202 but no scriptable content (likely JS-rendered); included on the strength of the AE directory alone.
- Several founding years are inferred from "N years" / "Nth anniversary" phrasing on org sites (Help Autism, Luxembourg's FAL, Ukraine's Child With Future, Turkey's Denizli) rather than an explicit stated date — flagged individually in each entry's notes.

**Leads not verified in time:** Georgia, Armenia, North Macedonia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, and Montenegro have no listing in Autism-Europe's directory and no web-search access was available this session to locate independent national autism societies — worth a follow-up pass once search quota resets. Azerbaijan's AE-affiliated member ("Together and Healthy Public Union") never resolved (`konfrans.birgesaglam.az`). A Czech self-advocacy group referenced elsewhere as "Autistik" could not be traced to a live domain and was dropped rather than guessed.
