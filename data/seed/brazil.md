# Brazil — seed data report

15 entries, all Brazil (BR), across 10 cities/states: Fortaleza, Brasília, São Paulo (4), Porto Alegre, Florianópolis, Goiânia, Rio de Janeiro (3), Ribeirão Preto, Salvador, Belo Horizonte.

By type: advocacy (5), community (4), education (2), media (2), work (1), arts (1). By leadership: nd-led (5), ally-led (4), mixed (2), unknown (4, left unguessed where sources didn't state it: ABDA, Specialisterne Brasil, Mad in Brasil, UFSC project).

## Uncertain entries

- **ABDA** — tdah.org.br would not respond from this network (DNS resolves, connection times out) despite being active elsewhere (its YouTube channel, USP materials). Used the YouTube channel as `source_url` and flagged the discrepancy.
- **MOAB** — moab.org.br no longer resolves; verified via Wikipedia and an active Instagram instead. Its own materials describe it as family-founded, not autistic-led, despite the "pride" branding.
- **Coletivo Autista UFRGS** — real, covered by UFRGS's own newspaper, but I couldn't confirm whether autistic students or allies run it day to day, beyond belonging to the CAUSP-inspired network.
- **Movimento Nacional da Luta Antimanicomial** — genuine national movement, no fixed HQ or website; located at Salvador (its founding 1993 meeting) as a proxy. Facebook page exists but returned 400 on every check here.
- **Festival Acessa BH** — strongest disability-arts festival found, explicitly disabled-led, but broader disability culture alongside neurodivergence, not neurodiversity-specific.

## Promising leads not verified in time

- **Autistas Brasil** (autistas.org.br) — a real, significant national autistic-led umbrella group (cited in a Gazeta do Povo piece on government lobbying), but no headquarters city or founding date confirmable (about/contact pages 403'd or 404'd); dropped rather than invent coordinates.
- **Coletivo Autista da UFRJ** and **da Unicamp** — both in press coverage of the post-CAUSP wave of collectives, but no dedicated site or social handle confirmably theirs.
- **CooTEA** — dropped once its parent org "Adapte" turned out to run on Applied Behavior Analysis, which the brief asks to avoid.
- **Coletivo Stim UFPR** (Curitiba) — Instagram account nearly inactive, not enough to confirm it's a going concern.
- **Festival "Viva As Diferenças"** (Recife) — real neurodiversity-themed festival, organized by a private autism clinic with no independent web presence found.

## Process note

Portuguese search was used early, before this session's search budget ran out; the rest relied on WebFetch/curl against known outlets, university news sites, and Wikipedia. Curitiba, Manaus, and Belém are likely under-covered as a result, not from a lack of relevant organizations.
