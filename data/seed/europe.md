18 entries, 12 countries: United Kingdom (5, the cap), Czechia (2), Portugal (2), and one each from France, Germany, Netherlands, Belgium, Italy, Ireland, Spain, Norway, Denmark.

Types: advocacy (5), community (7), arts (1), work (2), education (1), media (1), research (1). Fourteen of eighteen are nd-led or mixed; Dislex, PASPARTA and the anchor Specialisterne Foundation are marked "ally-led" rather than guessed upward.

## Uncertain or worth a second look

- **AMATDAH** (Madrid, ADHD) — every automated check returned HTTP 429, which reads as bot-protection, not downtime (SID-INICO and SOM360 both confirm it's active). Worth a manual browser check before publishing.
- **Aspies e.V.** (Berlin) — curl failed with a TLS renegotiation error against this one domain specifically; a separate fetch tool pulled the live page fine. Treated as up.
- **Brainstorm** (Portugal, ADHD) — couldn't find a physical city; it reads as fully online and nationwide. Lisbon is a placeholder anchor, not a verified location.
- **Hearing Voices Network (England)** — no founding year confirmable on the current site (commonly cited elsewhere as circa 1988, but that's not on-site so I left it null).
- **Collectif Autiste de Belgique** — solid and active, but no founding year stated anywhere on the site.
- **EUCAP** — legally registered in Prague but is a pan-European umbrella of 27 organisations in 18 countries; a Czech map pin is accurate as a legal address but could read as misleadingly national. Flagged in notes.
